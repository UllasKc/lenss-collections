LensS Routing Reference

Every rule that decides where an Assistant question goes, how deep it is answered, and what Genie is sent with it, written from the code. Rule IDs (like R7) are used in the diagrams and tables. The right-hand column of each table names the function that implements the rule.

Three destinations: LensS guide questions about the app, answered from the platform guide · Genie Quick Genie's Chat mode, one query, ~20–30 s · Genie Deep Genie's Agent mode, several queries and reasoning, 1–3 min.

6. Router model

What it is given

  • A fixed prompt describing the two destinations, two depths and six intents, with examples ("Where is the risk concentrated?" is data; "Where is the DPD filter?" is the guide).
  • The chat so far from historyWindow(): the whole chat when it is under 2,000 tokens; otherwise the stored summary, the newest whole question-and-answer pairs within 2,000 tokens, and one-line summaries for older turns the summary doesn't reach. Each turn says who answered it ("the LensS guide", "a quick answer", "a saved quick answer", "a deep analysis").
  • The latest message (first 1,000 characters), with personal details already masked.

What it returns

  • JSON: destination (data | platform), depth (quick | deep), intent, standalone (the request rewritten to stand alone, ≤ 40 words), confidence 0–1, reason.
  • Up to 220 tokens of output, timeout 8 s (auto_mode.timeout_ms).
  • If the reply has no valid destination or depth, or the call fails or times out: no reading, and the word rules decide (fallback is logged). An unknown intent becomes new_question; a missing confidence becomes 0.5.
IntentMeaning (as told to the model)Example
new_questionA fresh question"Which products are behind target?"
follow_upContinues the conversation, including a new question about something in the answer"and for Mumbai?", "why is the third one so high?"
more_depthOnly when the person says the answer was not enough or asks to go deeper on the same thing"This is not enough, I need more info"
repeatAsks again something already asked in this chatThe same question, or the same in other words
pushbackRejects the previous answer"No, you do it", "that's not what I asked"
platform_helpAbout the app itself"How do I export?"

Always whole question-and-answer pairs, newest first, never cut mid-sentence (except the latest pair past its cap, cut at a paragraph and labelled). Tokens are estimated at about 4 characters each.

12. Genie conversations

Genie remembers only its own conversation, with every table, query and step it produced. Each chat in the sidebar holds two, one per mode, stored on the chat (genie_conversation_id, agent_conversation_id).

21. Questions and answers

I said "No, you do it" after a guide answer. Where does it go?
If the guide's answer was about the app (like "Where is the DPD filter?"), it stays with the guide (D5b), because the data can't operate the app. If the guide answered a data question by mistake, it goes to the data with the original question (D6, W2). If your message names data ("show me the DPD numbers"), it goes to the data.
Does Regenerate start a deep analysis?
No. Regenerate and Try again re-ask the same question in the same mode (E10, E11). Only typing the same question again is read as "asked again" (R2, R3).
Why did my question go to the guide instead of the data?
The router model chose the guide with at least 70% confidence (D2), and your message either had wording about the app (S4) or named something on screen (D3). If it was wrong, press Answer from the data instead, or push back ("no, show me the data"): pushback never gets the guide twice (D6). The trace shows the exact reason.
Why did a question take 1–3 minutes?
It went to a Deep analysis: you chose Deep (M3), Auto judged it needed reasoning (M4), you asked for more or pushed back (R3, M2), or you asked again after a weak answer (R3). The route line says which.
I chose Quick. Why did I get a Deep analysis?
Your latest message asked for more ("not enough", "go deeper"), pushed back on a data answer, or repeated a question whose answer was weak. Your message outranks the setting (M2). A new question about the answer ("why is the third one so high?") stays Quick (I2).
What happens if I ask exactly the same question again?
The router finds the earlier question (S1). If its answer was weak (K1–K5), a fresh deep analysis starts at once (R3). If it was good, you choose: Run a deep analysis or Show the earlier answer (R2, U8).
Does Genie remember earlier questions?
Yes, within its own conversation for that mode in that chat (L1–L3), including every table and query. Answers it never gave (cache, guide, the other mode) are sent to it as missed turns (C3), within 2,500 tokens.
When does Genie start a new conversation?
New chat or Ask LensS button (L4), first use of a mode in a chat (L5), a busy retry (L6, L7), or when the first question in a mode failed (L8).
Is the router's rewritten question sent to Genie?
Only when your message points back at a guide answer, such as "No, you do it" (W2). Otherwise Genie gets your own words; the rewrite is kept in the trace.
Are guide questions and answers ever sent to Genie?
No (C2). They are sent to the router and the guide's own model, which use them to understand follow-ups.
What if the router model is down or slow?
After 8 s, or on an unreadable reply, the word rules decide on the signals alone (D4, M5, section 8). Anything uncertain goes to the data. The trace and the routing panel show "decided by the word rules".
Do clicked suggestions go through the router?
No. They go straight to Genie in their own mode, or to the guide when their wording is about the app (F1). "Go deeper" and "Answer from the data instead" are fixed too (F2, F3).
Why didn't I get an instant cached answer to my follow-up?
Only the first question of a chat, a suggestion, or a refresh can use the cache (Q1). Follow-ups depend on the conversation, so they always go live. Escalated questions never use it (Q2).
Why did I see a "Go deeper" button?
The Quick answer was thin (a line or two, a question back, little text without a chart; U5), or the quality check later scored its completeness below 70% (U6). It appears only on the latest answer.
How much of the chat does each part see?
Router: 2,000 tokens; guide: 1,000; Genie: only what it missed, 2,500 (latest pair up to 4,000). The whole chat when it is under 2,000. All are settings (section 11).
Are personal details ever sent to the router or Genie?
No. They are masked before the router's model sees the question and before anything is stored or sent (G1).
Where can I check how a specific question was routed?
Observability → Pipeline traces → select the question → stage 1 "Ask", or "Inspect in Observability ›" under the answer. Section 16 lists every logged field.
How do I test routing after a change?
Observability → Evaluations → tick Routing → Run evals (about 40 s, router model only). For a code change, also run the offline rule tests and the smoke test.

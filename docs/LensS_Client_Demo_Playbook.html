The pitch in one breath

LensS turns governed collections data into a decision you can act on today: where you stand, why, which customers to call, and an AI assistant whose every answer is checked, explained and audited.

Decide, not just report

The Command Center reads as one story, from "are we on track?" to "which 610 promises to save today". Every number opens the exact accounts behind it.

Ask in plain language

The Assistant answers questions about the data in about 20 seconds, or runs a multi-step analysis in 1 to 3 minutes, and remembers the conversation.

Trust you can show an auditor

Every figure comes from certified views. Every AI answer shows its SQL, is scored for faithfulness, passes guardrails and lands in an audit trail.

Say early, once: the portfolio on screen is a sample data pack (Indian rupees, one month-to-date snapshot), not any client's data.

Questions you will get

Is the AI making up these numbers?
No. Every figure on the Command Center and Explorer is SQL on certified views; no AI computes them. In the Assistant, each answer shows the SQL it ran, and a faithfulness judge checks the figures in the answer against the query results (Observability → Answer quality).
Does our data leave our environment?
LensS is built on Databricks: Unity Catalog for governed data, Genie for questions, Databricks-served models for checks and summaries, Lakebase for chat history, and a Databricks App for the interface. It is designed to be deployed in the client's own workspace. Confirm any specific security or residency requirement with the solution architect.
What about customer personal data?
The demo data holds account IDs only, no names or contact details. Guardrails mask personal data in questions before they reach the engine and in answers before they are shown. A question asking for customer names and phone numbers is refused (it's in the automated test suite).
Can you forecast next month?
Not honestly from a single snapshot, and LensS says so instead of guessing. The outlook shown is a pipeline view for this month: collected so far plus promises due, at the rate promises are being kept. With monthly history loaded, trend and forecast views become possible.
Which strategy works best? Can it prove uplift?
It compares strategies like-for-like (same product, arrears stage, balance band and vulnerability), but that is observational. Proving causal uplift needs a champion/challenger test with random assignment; LensS states that limit rather than claiming a number.
Where do the rules come from (priority accounts, next steps)?
From the business rules in the data pack, for example priority = non-payment risk ≥ 0.70 and payment propensity ≥ 0.25. Thresholds are configurable, and because each rule lives in one certified view, a change applies everywhere at once.
How long to set up on our data? What does it cost?
Don't quote either in the room. Say: "It depends on your data model and environment; we'd scope that in a short discovery," and take it offline with the account lead.
Does it only work for collections?
Collections is the first use case built. The pattern (certified views, a story-driven command center, a governed assistant with full observability) is general; whether and how it applies to another domain is a discussion for discovery.
What if an answer is wrong?
Each answer carries a quality score and its SQL, users can give thumbs up or down, and low scores and negative feedback appear in Observability for review. Suggested questions are pre-answered and checked, and refreshed whenever the data changes.

Before you go live

  • Deploy the latest version the day before, and check the deploy log ends with the app URL and no errors.
  • Confirm the 10 suggested questions are pre-answered: in the app's Logs, look for pre-warm finished: 10/10 answered.
  • Open the app 10 minutes early, so the database is warm and the Command Center loads instantly; click through all four tabs once.
  • Check the Command Center numbers match this playbook (₹53.4M, ₹59.2M, 610, 604). If they differ, use the screen; the story is the same.
  • Clear old test chats from the Assistant's history, and start a fresh chat.
  • Set the browser zoom to 100% and close other tabs; on a large monitor the pages use the full width.
  • Have a backup: screenshots of each chapter and one completed deep analysis, in case the network fails.

Lines to avoid

  • "It predicts…" or "it forecasts next month". It doesn't; it shows a pipeline outlook for this month.
  • "Switching to strategy X will lift recovery by Y%". Comparisons are observational, not causal.
  • "This is real customer data." It is a sample portfolio.
  • Any timeline, price or named reference client. Take those offline.
  • "The AI calculates the KPIs." Certified SQL does; the AI explains and answers questions.
  • Reading every number aloud. Read each chapter's bold first line, then click one "View accounts".
